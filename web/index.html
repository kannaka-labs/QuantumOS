How this works

        

          This page runs an unmodified qemu-system-x86_64, compiled to
          WebAssembly (ktock/qemu-wasm),
          booting QuantumOS's multiboot kernel. Your keystrokes go to the emulated
          serial port; the OS never leaves your tab.
        

      

      

        
Honest entropy

        

          There's no quantum hardware in a browser, so the “quantum” dice and seeds
          here come from a deterministic PRNG — the boot log says so:
          noise source = prng. On real quantum backends QuantumOS draws
          true entropy; in your tab it's an honest stand-in.
